Liberation App Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Introduction

Liberation is a personal recovery support application designed to help users reflect on their experiences, build healthy routines, maintain journals, explore emotions, and work through recovery-related activities.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Liberation app.

2. Information You Enter

Depending on the features you use, the app may allow you to enter information such as:

  • Journal entries and personal reflections.
  • Recovery goals, tasks, and progress.
  • Daily check-ins and emotional reflections.
  • Recovery dates and step-related activities.
  • App preferences, themes, and settings.

Some of this information may be personal or sensitive. Please avoid entering information you do not want stored on your device.

3. Storage and Data Handling

The way information is stored and processed depends on the app's implementation and the features you use. The developer must verify whether the app stores information locally, transmits it to external services, or uses third-party software that processes data.

The developer does not intend to sell users' personal information.

4. Data Sharing

Liberation does not intentionally share personal journal entries or recovery reflections with other users. Any data processed by third-party services, if applicable, must be disclosed accurately in the app's Google Play Data safety declaration and this Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Users should review the app's available data management features, including any archive, restore, or deletion options. Uninstalling the app may remove locally stored information, but it may not remove information stored elsewhere, if applicable.

6. Children's Privacy

Liberation is not specifically designed for children. The developer must configure the app's target audience and apply the relevant age-related requirements.

7. Security

The developer aims to handle user information responsibly. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Health and Recovery Disclaimer

Liberation is a self-reflection and recovery support tool. It is not a substitute for professional medical care, mental health treatment, diagnosis, or emergency assistance.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the app developer through the contact information provided on the Liberation Google Play listing.
